# Code of conduct

Qasa ERP is built for, and with, people who speak Arabic, Kurdish and English and who come from many backgrounds.
Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other space of this project) is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people writing in their second or third language;
- assume good intent, and criticise ideas and code, never people;
- welcome questions from beginners, accountants and developers alike;
- keep discussions on the topic of the project.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination for any reason (including language, ethnicity, religion,
gender or nationality), threats, sharing someone's private information, and spam.

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and may block people who
repeat them. To report a problem, email **info@qasaerp.com**; reports are handled confidentially.

**Qasa ERP 0.1.2 for Windows** adds the final accounts, returns, opening stock and transfers between warehouses. Installed copies update themselves.

**Download:** `Qasa-ERP-Setup.exe` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit). Also at [qasaerp.com](https://qasaerp.com/#download).

### What's new
- **Final accounts** (الحسابات الختامية): the trading account, the current operations account, the profit and loss account and the balance sheet, in the layout of the Iraqi Unified Accounting System, with sub-accounts on request. Viewing is free; printing and Excel export are part of Pro.
- **Sales and purchase returns:** open a posted invoice and choose **Return items**. Only what is left on each line can come back. The refund goes back in cash or off the customer's or supplier's balance, with the invoice's discount shared fairly. Goods return to stock at their original cost.
- **Opening stock:** enter the goods on hand when you start with Qasa ERP, at their cost, against capital.
- **Transfers between warehouses** at average cost (with Pro, which allows more than one warehouse).
- Each return and stock document has its own number (SR, PR, OS, ST) and can be cancelled with a reversing entry.

### Your company file
When 0.1.2 first opens your company file, it upgrades it to the new format. Everything you entered stays as it is. Keep a copy first if you like: **File → Open data folder**.

### When you install
Our digital signature is still being issued. Until then Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" or "Unknown publisher": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

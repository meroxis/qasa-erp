**Qasa ERP 0.1.5 for Windows** brings the office network, automatic backups and year-end closing. Installed copies update themselves.

**Download:** `Qasa-ERP-Setup.exe` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit). Also at [qasaerp.com](https://qasaerp.com/#download).

### What's new
- **Office network (Pro):** other PCs in the office work on one PC's company file at the same time, each person with their own user. Switch it on in **Settings → Office network**; on the other PCs choose **File → Connect to an office server…** and type the address and pairing code it shows. The connection is encrypted, and each PC accepts only that office server.
- **Automatic backups (Pro):** a checked copy of the company file every day, in a folder you choose (OneDrive, Google Drive or a USB disk), keeping the last 30. **Settings → Backups** also restores any copy; the current file is kept aside, never deleted. Restoring works on every plan.
- **Encrypted backups:** set a backup password and the copies are encrypted, so a cloud folder only holds copies nobody can read. Keep the password safe: it can't be recovered.
- **Year-end closing (Pro):** once a year has ended, **Settings → Year-end closing** moves revenue and expenses into the accumulated surplus (deficit) account 229 and locks the year's months. The year's final accounts keep its result, and a year can be reopened with a reversal.
- **Buy online:** the Plan & license page opens our customer portal to buy or renew a license.

### When you install
Our digital signature is still being issued. Until then Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" or "Unknown publisher": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The first time the office network is switched on, Windows may ask whether Qasa ERP may use the network: allow it on private networks.

**Qasa ERP 0.1.1 for Windows** adds plans and licenses. Installed copies update themselves.

**Download:** `Qasa-ERP-Setup.exe` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit). Also at [qasaerp.com](https://qasaerp.com/#download).

### What's new
- **Plan & license** page: see your plan, compare Free, Pro and Business, and activate a license key. Keys work offline.
- **Free stays free**, with no time limit: all accounting, invoices and stock for one company on one PC, with one warehouse.
- **Try Pro free for 30 days** from inside the app, once per company, without a key.
- Pro and Business remove "Made with Qasa ERP" from invoices and allow more warehouses. More Pro features are coming and are marked as such.
- **Your data is never locked:** when a license ends, the app keeps working for 14 more days with a reminder, then continues on Free with everything you entered.
- Email links in the app now open your mail program.

### When you install
Our digital signature is still being issued. Until then Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" or "Unknown publisher": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

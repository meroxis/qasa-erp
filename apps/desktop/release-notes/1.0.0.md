### Licences from 0.1.8: connect once in the first month
- **If you entered a Pro or Business licence in 0.1.8 or earlier, connect this PC to the internet at least once during your first month on 1.0.** Licences are now activated online on each PC.
- Your licence keeps working for 30 days from the first start of 1.0, and it activates by itself as soon as the PC is online. Nothing else to do.
- If it is still not activated after those 30 days, the company runs on the Free plan until you activate it (Plan & license → **Activate now**). Your data always stays as it is.
- **In an office network,** the PC that keeps the company file is the one to connect. If activation is refused because the licence is already active on all the PCs it allows, free an old PC at my.qasaerp.com or contact us.

### New
- **Pages for your kind of business** (Pro): mobile shop, car parts, engineering works, electrical & solar and generator owners; real estate with the unit map and reservation deposits (Business). You choose the business type when you first start.
- **Payroll** (Pro): the monthly payroll with allowances, deductions and social security, advances taken from the salary, and printed payslips.
- **Point of sale** with shifts, **quotations**, the **sales pipeline** (Pro), expenses and the **Daily Brief**, sent to WhatsApp with one tap.
- **Banks & cheques:** bank accounts, transfers and cheques with reminders before they fall due.
- **Fixed assets** with depreciation, stock adjustments and stock counts, purchase orders, and wholesale and customer price lists.
- **Print design:** your logo and letterhead on invoices and vouchers, and **barcode labels**.
- **Branches** (Business), and **several companies on one PC** (Free 1, Pro 3, Business as many as you need).
- **Cloud backup** to Google Drive and OneDrive, encrypted before it leaves the PC (Pro).
- **The office network from anywhere through Qasa Relay:** work from home or another branch with no public IP and no router setup (an activated Pro or Business licence).
- **Connect your website or online shop:** the website API and the WooCommerce plugin keep prices and stock up to date and bring orders in as draft invoices (Pro).
- **Activation on this PC:** a licence is activated online once, and can be moved to another PC from Plan & license.
- **The first month on each PC is free**, with every feature except Qasa Relay. It starts by itself.
- **Choose your language on the first screen:** Arabic, Kurdish or English, before anything else.
- **The user guide** on qasaerp.com, step by step in three languages: Help → User guide, or the "?" beside a page title.
- Also: the dollar rate board, filled in for each day; a signed QR code on every invoice; item pictures; Excel import; WhatsApp sharing; update notices and a beta channel.

### Security
- Licences are signed and activated for each PC, and the licence checks run as compiled code.
- Passwords the app keeps (backup, database server, cloud and Qasa Relay sign-ins) are encrypted for your Windows user.
- Website orders reach the office sealed with the office's own key.
- The app's server runs in a process of its own, so the window stays responsive on large books.
- Electron and every library updated to their patched versions.

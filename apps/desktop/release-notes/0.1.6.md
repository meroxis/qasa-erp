**Qasa ERP 0.1.6 for Windows** fixes the office network's connect window. Installed copies update themselves.

**Download:** `Qasa-ERP-Setup.exe` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit). Also at [qasaerp.com](https://qasaerp.com/#download).

### Fixed
- **File → Connect to an office server…** opened an empty window in 0.1.5. It now shows the address and pairing code fields and connects.

### When you install
Our digital signature is still being issued. Until then Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" or "Unknown publisher": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

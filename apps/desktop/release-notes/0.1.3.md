**Qasa ERP 0.1.3 for Windows** adds users, sign-in and roles. Installed copies update themselves.

**Download:** `Qasa-ERP-Setup.exe` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit). Also at [qasaerp.com](https://qasaerp.com/#download).

### What's new
- **Users and sign-in** (System → Users). Nothing changes until you switch sign-in on: Qasa ERP keeps opening as the owner. Set your password, switch sign-in on, and everyone signs in with their own name and password. Their name goes on the vouchers they make, and every sign-in is recorded.
- **Roles:** admin, preparer (المنظم), checker (المدقق), approver (المصادق), sales, purchasing, storekeeper and viewer. Each person sees only the buttons their roles allow, and Qasa ERP refuses everything else.
- **Separate duties** (Pro): whoever prepared a voucher can neither check nor approve it.
- The Free plan has one user, who can protect Qasa ERP with a password. Pro adds up to 5 users. The office network, for several PCs on one company file, is coming next.
- Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. After five wrong passwords in a row, the account waits before the next try.

### Your company file
When 0.1.3 first opens your company file, it upgrades it. Everything you entered stays as it is.

### When you install
Our digital signature is still being issued. Until then Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" or "Unknown publisher": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

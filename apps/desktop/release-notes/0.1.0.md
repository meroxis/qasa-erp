The first preview of **Qasa ERP for Windows** — accounting software for Iraqi companies, built on the Iraqi Unified Accounting System.

**Download:** `Qasa-ERP-Setup.exe` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit). Also at [qasaerp.com](https://qasaerp.com/#download).

### What's in it
- The unified chart of accounts in Arabic, English and Kurdish
- Receipt and payment vouchers and journal entries — prepared → checked → approved, gap-free numbers, reversals, period locking, audit log
- Sales and purchase invoices, customers and suppliers with statements, items and warehouses at average cost
- Trial balance and account statements, print and Excel export
- Dinars and dollars at the day's rate
- Automatic updates: the app checks this page, downloads new versions in the background and asks before restarting

### When you install
Our digital signature is being issued. Until then Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" or "Unknown publisher": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Your company file is kept at `%APPDATA%\Qasa ERP\data\qasa.sqlite`.

**Qasa ERP 0.1.8 for Windows** brings installment sales with guarantors, and can keep the company's books on the company's own MariaDB or MySQL server. Installed copies update themselves.

**Download:** `Qasa-ERP-Setup.exe` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit). Also at [qasaerp.com](https://qasaerp.com/#download).

### New
- **Installment sales** (Pro plan, and the 30-day trial): Sales → Installments.
  - Make a contract from a posted credit sale (the sale's page has a button for it), or for a balance a customer owed before Qasa ERP.
  - Set the down payment, the number of months and the first due date. The schedule is shown before you save, with round monthly amounts.
  - Add a guarantor with their phone, national ID number and workplace. The Guarantors page shows how much each one stands for.
  - **Collect payment** posts a receipt voucher at once and marks the installments paid, oldest first. Reversing that voucher takes the payment back.
  - See at a glance what is due this week, what is overdue, and what was collected this month. The aging report groups late installments by 30, 60, 90 and more days.
  - Print the contract with its schedule and the guarantor's pledge, or send the customer a WhatsApp reminder.
  - A sale under an active contract can't be cancelled or returned until the contract is cancelled. A contract's terms, schedule and collections never change.
- **Company database on a MariaDB or MySQL server** (Business plan): Settings → Company database → Move the company to a database server. It works with MariaDB 10.6 or newer and MySQL 8.0.16 or newer.
  - The whole company is copied into an empty database and checked row by row before anything switches. This PC's company file is kept aside, untouched.
  - The other PCs keep connecting to this PC through the office network. The server lets only one Qasa ERP work on a database at a time.
  - The connection is always encrypted. The server's certificate is checked before the password is sent: a public certificate, one from the server's own authority (MySQL's `ca.pem`), or the self-signed certificate you confirm by its fingerprint (MariaDB). The password is kept on this PC, protected for your Windows user.
  - **Move the company back to a file on this PC** at any time. Backups keep working and are still company files.

### When you install
Our digital signature is still being issued. Until then Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" or "Unknown publisher": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

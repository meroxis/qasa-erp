**Qasa ERP 0.1.4 for Windows** gets ready for licenses bought online, with a few fixes. Installed copies update themselves.

**Download:** `Qasa-ERP-Setup.exe` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit). Also at [qasaerp.com](https://qasaerp.com/#download).

### What's new
- **License keys from our customer portal** work in Qasa ERP, next to the keys Meroxis issues by hand. Nothing changes for a license you already activated.

### Fixed
- Names and notes typed in one language keep their own word order in lists shown in the other direction.
- On invoices, the customer's phone number sits under the name.
- The trial balance says whether the *balance* is balanced (it said "entry").
- The last sign-in of a user shows the local date.
- The plans table lists users as available and the office network as coming.

### When you install
Our digital signature is still being issued. Until then Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" or "Unknown publisher": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
